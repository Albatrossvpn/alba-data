Albatross for browsers: privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026 (version 1.1)

Albatross for browsers is a browser extension that works with the Albatross VPN app installed on the same computer. This policy covers the extension only; the app has its own privacy notes on albatrossvpn.online.

The short version

The extension talks only to the Albatross app on your own computer. It has no analytics, no tracking and no advertising, and it sends nothing to Albatross's servers or to anyone else.

What the extension stores

In your browser's extension storage, on your computer only:

  • the pairing key the Albatross app gave this browser when you paired it (a random value that lets the extension ask the app for its state);
  • your settings: "Protect this browser", WebRTC leak protection, "Browser only", notifications, "Match time zone and location", "Show each tab's route" and the theme;
  • your site rules (sites you set to use the VPN, to open directly, or to ask), your favourite locations and your last five locations;
  • the port the app answered on, and the list of your region's sites the app opens directly, so that they keep working while the app is not running.

For the current browser session only (cleared when the browser closes): your answers to "Ask" sites that you did not ask Albatross to remember, and the last state the popup showed, so it opens at once.

Nothing else. The extension does not store your browsing history, page contents, passwords, cookies or form data.

What the extension sends, and to whom

Only to the Albatross app on your own computer (the address 127.0.0.1): the pairing key, and your requests to connect, disconnect, change location, list locations or bring the app's window up. Its content security policy forbids its pages to connect anywhere else.

When "Protect this browser" is on, or a site is set to "Use VPN", that traffic goes through the Albatross app, which carries it to the VPN server it connected to. What the app and the servers can and cannot see is explained in the app's Privacy sheet and on albatrossvpn.online.

What the extension reads

  • The current tab's address, when you open the popup, use the page's right-click menu or press a shortcut, to show and set that site's rule.
  • If you set a site to "Ask" or turn on "Show each tab's route" (and allow it): the addresses your tabs open, to ask before such a site loads and to show each tab's route on the toolbar. They are compared with your rules in the browser and never stored or sent.
  • It never reads page contents.

Match time zone and location (off unless you turn it on)

With this on, while your browser goes through the VPN, a small script runs in each page before the page's own scripts. It makes the page see the VPN country's time zone and that country's capital as your location. It reads nothing on the page and sends nothing anywhere. Your real location is never asked for while it is on. Sites you set to open directly keep your real time zone.

Notifications

If you leave them on, the extension shows a notification when the VPN drops without you asking, and when it is back. They are made in your browser; nothing is sent.

Analytics and advertising

None. No analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no third-party code.

Removing your data

Remove the browser in the Albatross app (Settings, Browsers) to cancel its pairing key at once, or choose "Unpair this browser" in the extension. Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored.
