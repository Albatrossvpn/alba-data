Seeing a warning?

If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway. The installer is not code-signed yet.

To check the file: in File Explorer, open Downloads, type cmd in the address bar and press Enter, then run certutil -hashfile Albatross-Setup-<version>.exe SHA256. The 64 characters it prints must match the SHA-256 above.

Steps

Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit. You need an Albatross account: a username and a password, nothing else. Get a free trial account, or buy one.

  1. Click Download for Windows above. On a slow connection, take the same file from GitHub.
  2. Run the installer. If Windows says Windows protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway: the installer is not code-signed yet.
  3. Open Albatross, sign in with your username and password, and click the big button.
  4. Leave the location on Fastest location and the connection setting on Automatic. To pick a location yourself, you need a Silver plan or higher.

Protect the whole device, start with Windows

  • By default Albatross sets the system proxy, which browsers and most apps use. If a game or an app does not go through it, turn on Protect the whole device in Settings (Gold plans and up). Windows asks once for administrator rights.
  • To open Albatross every time you sign in to Windows, turn on Start with system. With Auto-connect on too, it connects by itself.
  • To turn it off, click the big button again, or choose Disconnect or Quit in the menu of its tray icon.
  • Do not run another VPN at the same time.

Share with TVs and consoles

A TV, an Xbox or a PlayStation cannot run a VPN app. Your Windows PC or laptop can share its connection with them, in one of two ways. If a switch shows a lock, the app tells you which plan has it.

Share with TVs and consoles (websites and streaming apps)

  1. Connect the PC and the TV or console to the same home network (Wi-Fi or cable).
  2. In Albatross, open Settings and turn on Share with TVs and consoles. Under the switch, the app shows the address to use.
  3. On the TV or console, open its network settings and set the DNS by hand to that address. Leave everything else automatic.
  4. If the TV cannot connect: Windows must call this network Private, and Albatross must be allowed in the firewall. If the PC's address changes, turn the switch off and on again.

Windows' Mobile hotspot (everything, games too)

  1. In Albatross, turn on Share through the hotspot. It turns on Protect the whole device as well.
  2. In Windows Settings, open Network and internet, Mobile hotspot, and turn it on.
  3. Connect the Xbox, PlayStation or TV to the hotspot's Wi-Fi name with its password. Nothing else needs to be set on it.

Good to know

  • Updates install from inside the app, over the version you have.
