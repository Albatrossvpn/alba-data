Android TV

Set up a VPN on Android TV and Google TV.

For TVs, boxes and sticks with Android TV or Google TV. Install the app once, then turn it on with the remote.

Download

TVs, boxes and sticks take their own APK, the 32-bit one (armv7), which also runs on 64-bit TVs. Phones keep the usual APK.

Seeing a warning?

If the TV says Downloader may not install apps, open Settings and turn on Allow from this source for Downloader, then choose Install again. If Play Protect warns about the app, choose More details, then Install anyway.

Steps with Downloader

Android TV 6 and newer, and Google TV. You need an Albatross account: a username and a password, nothing else. Buy one in our Telegram bot, or on your account page with USDT.

  1. On the TV, open Google Play and install Downloader (by AFTVnews).
  2. In the TV's settings, allow Downloader to install apps: Settings, Apps (or Device preferences, Security and restrictions), Install unknown apps, then turn on Downloader.
  3. Open Downloader, type albatrossvpn.online/android-tv and open this page. Choose Download the TV APK (armv7).
  4. When the download ends, choose Install. You can delete the downloaded file afterwards.
  5. Open Albatross from the TV's apps: it has its own tile on the TV's launcher. On Google TV it is under Apps, Your apps; on Android TV, in the apps row of the home screen.
  6. Sign in with your username and password, press the big button, and choose OK when the TV asks to allow the VPN.

With a USB stick

  1. On a computer, download the TV APK (armv7) from this page and copy it to a USB stick.
  2. Put the stick in the TV or box and open it with a file manager (for example File Commander or X-plore from Google Play).
  3. Open the APK, allow the file manager to install apps when the TV asks, and choose Install.

With the remote

  • The arrows move between buttons, switches and fields, and a ring shows where you are; the middle button presses. Back closes a screen and comes back to Connect.
  • To type your username and password, select the field and use the on-screen keyboard. Some remotes have a microphone button; spell the password with the keyboard instead.
  • Turn on Auto-connect in Settings, so Albatross connects when it starts, and again by itself after the TV restarts or the app updates.
  • On a TV the app leaves out what only phones have: the map, Override GPS and hotspot sharing.

Always-on VPN (advanced, optional)

On Android TV the system hides the Always-on VPN switch and its kill switch; in the app, Settings, Kill switch says the same. Albatross still connects again by itself after a restart if it was connected or Auto-connect is on. If you know adb, you can turn the system's Always-on VPN on from a computer on the same network, with the TV's developer options and network debugging on:

  • adb shell settings put secure always_on_vpn_app app.albatross.vpn makes Albatross the always-on VPN.
  • adb shell settings put secure always_on_vpn_lockdown 1 also blocks the internet while the VPN is off. Leave this one out if you are not sure.

Not needed for everyday use: with Auto-connect on, Albatross connects when it opens and after the TV restarts.

Good to know

  • The TV, box or stick needs Android TV or Google TV. An LG or Samsung TV has another system: see TVs without a VPN app.
  • Later updates install from inside the app.
