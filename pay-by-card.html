Payments

Pay by card.

Albatross is paid for with Telegram Stars in our Telegram bot, or on your account page with USDT or another coin, or by card where your account page offers it. Plans and prices are shown in our Telegram bot and on your account page after you sign in. If your account page offers no card payment, a bank card that is accepted on international websites can still pay in two steps: it buys USDT, and the USDT pays on your account page. We never take card details and never see your card.

Before you start

  • You need an Albatross account. No account yet? Get one on your account page, with no Telegram needed: it starts when your first payment arrives.
  • Look at the price first. On your account page, under Pay with crypto, each package shows its price in USDT. Services that sell crypto for cards charge a fee, often a few percent with a minimum charge, and many have a minimum purchase. A small package can cost much more by card than by other ways, so card suits the larger packages best. Check what you will receive, after the fee, before you pay.
  • Use a card that works on international websites. Some services do not accept cards from some countries.
  • Use the network the checkout offers. USDT on BNB Smart Chain (BEP20) comes first in its list because it usually costs the least to send. USDT sent on any other network than the one shown is lost.
  • A wallet of your own that holds USDT on that network, and a very small amount of the network's own coin (BNB on BNB Smart Chain) to pay the fee when you send. The wallet shows the fee before you send. Already have USDT in an exchange account? Skip the buying steps and withdraw it to the address in step 4; the exchange takes its fee out of what you withdraw, so make sure the full amount arrives.

Steps

  1. Choose your package. On your account page, open Pay with crypto and look at the packages. Do not press Get payment details yet: an order stays open only for a short time (the page shows how long), and buying with a card can take longer than that.
  2. Buy USDT with your card. Open a service that sells crypto for cards, such as Guardarian, Changelly or MoonPay. These are examples only: we have no tie to any of them and do not recommend one. Choose USDT on the BNB Smart Chain (BEP20) network and give it the address of your own wallet. Buy enough that, after the service's fee, the USDT that reaches your wallet is a little more than the package's price. Buy a very small amount of BNB for the fee as well; most services have a minimum purchase that is more than you need, and the rest stays in your wallet for next time. The service may ask you to confirm who you are: that is between you and the service.
  3. Wait for the USDT and the BNB to arrive in your wallet.
  4. Make the order. On your account page, in Pay with crypto, choose USDT on BNB Smart Chain (BEP20) and your package, and press Get payment details. The page shows an address and an exact amount. The last digits of the amount are a small extra that tells us the payment is yours, so use every digit.
  5. Send exactly that amount, in one payment, from your wallet to that address, on that network, before the time on the page runs out. The copy buttons next to the amount and the address avoid typing mistakes. If your wallet or exchange takes its fee out of the amount, add the fee so that the full amount arrives.
  6. Wait for the confirmation. The page updates by itself, and the data is added once the network confirms the payment, usually within a few minutes.

If something goes wrong

I paid, but the page is still waiting

Open I paid but it did not arrive under the order and paste the transaction ID, the long code your wallet or exchange shows for the payment. If we cannot match it ourselves, our team checks it.

Less than the full amount arrived, or it arrived after the time ran out

The order is not completed by itself. Write to us with your account name, the order number (it starts with C) and the transaction ID: use the chat on this page, or send /human to our Telegram bot. Our team checks it and tells you what can be done.

I sent it on a different network than the one shown

Write to us in the same way, but we may not be able to get it back. Check the network before every payment.

What we will never ask you for

We never ask for a private key, a seed phrase or a password, and you should never send them to anyone, in a chat or anywhere else. A transaction ID is safe to share.

Crypto payments cannot be reversed, so check the address, the network and the amount before you send.
