TVs and consoles

A VPN for your TV or console.

LG and Samsung TVs, Xbox and PlayStation cannot run VPN apps. Three ways that work.

What kind of TV do you have?

The name is on the TV's home screen, or in its settings under About.

Android TV or Google TV: install the app LG (webOS) Samsung (Tizen) Another TV, Xbox or PlayStation

Three ways that work

  1. An Android TV box or stick on the TV's HDMI port, such as a Google TV streamer or another Android TV box. Install Albatross on it with the Android TV guide and watch through it.
  2. Mirror your phone's screen with the VPN on. Connect Albatross on the phone, then mirror the whole screen to the TV. Mirror the whole screen: the Cast button inside an app makes the TV play the video by itself, outside the VPN. Some streaming apps do not allow mirroring.
  3. A Windows PC or laptop with Albatross: turn on Share with TVs and consoles and set the TV's DNS to the address the app shows, or share everything through Windows' Mobile hotspot. The Windows guide has the steps.

LG TVs (webOS)

  • Mirroring: on the TV, open Screen Share from the home screen. On an Android phone, open Screen mirroring, Smart View or Cast in the quick settings and pick the TV.
  • DNS from a Windows PC: on the TV, Settings, General (or Network), your Wi-Fi or wired connection, Edit or Advanced Wi-Fi settings, turn off Set automatically for DNS and enter the address.

Samsung TVs (Tizen)

  • Mirroring: on a Samsung phone, open Smart View in the quick settings and pick the TV. Other Android phones call it Screen mirroring or Cast.
  • DNS from a Windows PC: on the TV, Settings, General, Network, Network status, IP settings, set DNS setting to Enter manually and enter the address.

Xbox, PlayStation and other TVs

  • Websites and streaming apps: on a Windows PC with Albatross, turn on Share with TVs and consoles, then set the console's DNS by hand. On Xbox: Settings, General, Network settings, Advanced settings, DNS settings, Manual. On PlayStation: Settings, Network, Set up internet connection, your network, Advanced settings, DNS settings, Manual.
  • Games and everything else: on the PC, turn on Share through the hotspot in Albatross and Windows' Mobile hotspot, and connect the console to that hotspot.
  • A TV with Android TV or Google TV runs the app itself: see the Android TV guide.
