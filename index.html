Why Albatross

Built to connect, not to be configured.

Everything that usually takes a settings screen happens on its own.

Several servers per location, chosen automatically

Each location has several servers checked in the background, and the app can move to another one in the same location.

A location, picked for you

Albatross picks a location for you. On Silver and above, choose any location with servers available, each shown with its best latency.

Secure by default

Connections that would travel unencrypted are refused, so no one along the way can read them. Name lookups go through the tunnel too. On Android, one tap opens the system's always-on VPN switches.

One account, all your devices

One username and password works on Windows, Android, Mac, Linux, iPhone and iPad, up to your plan's device limit. No email, name or phone number.

Ad and tracker blocking

A list built every day from the major public filters plus regional ad networks, about half a million names, blocked at the name level with an allowlist that keeps sign-ins, payments and app stores working.

Local sites, direct

Sites in your country open directly instead of through the VPN, so banking, shopping and government services stay fast. Local sites missing from the built-in list are recognized while you browse, and open directly from then on.

Privacy

How Albatross treats your data.

What is protected on the way, and the little our account service sees.

What we doWhat Albatross does for you

  • Signing up needs only a username and password: no email, no phone number, no real name.
  • Traffic is encrypted from your device to the server; HTTPS sites stay encrypted to the site.
  • Unencrypted traffic is refused, so nothing the app carries can be read in plain text on the way. Name lookups for the sites you visit go through the tunnel.
  • The account service receives a username, a password at sign-in, and counts of bytes and seconds. It never receives the sites you visit.
  • “Learn from other users” is on by default. It shares anonymous counts only (which kinds of server work on your network, and how connections went): no server or device addresses, no sites, no username and no install number. Turn it off in Settings with one tap.
  • “Help others get online” (Windows, on by default) lets people in censored countries reach Tor through your computer, only while you are outside Iran on an open network. Up to four people can connect at once. They, the Tor Project and two public STUN servers can see your computer's internet address; none of them can see your files, your accounts or what you do online, and the sites they visit do not see your address. It uses some of your connection. Turn it off in Settings with one tap.
  • The support chat and the Telegram bot are answered by an AI assistant. Never write a password in either; the privacy policy says what is kept, and for how long.

Good to knowWhat a VPN cannot do

  • Your connection is carried through servers that may be run by independent parties. Treat any VPN server as able to see which sites you connect to and how much data you transfer.
  • A VPN protects you from your local network and provider. It does not make you anonymous.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Is Albatross free?

The app is free to download, but connecting needs an Albatross account: just a username and password, with no email, name or phone number. You buy one in our Telegram bot, with Telegram Stars or another way the bot offers, or on your account page with USDT. An account can have a data or time allowance, which the app and your account page show.

Do I have to choose a server?

No. You pick a location, or leave it on the fastest, and the app finds a working server for you.

Which connection setting should I choose?

Automatic. Albatross measures your network and picks what works on it right now, and changes it the moment that stops working.

Does it work on heavily filtered networks?

Networks differ, so we cannot promise it works on yours. Albatross tries several ways to connect, and on a heavily filtered network it can take up to a minute the first time.

How do I buy an account or add data?

In our Telegram bot with Telegram Stars: tap Buy data or send /buy, and a new account's username and password arrive in the same chat at once. Or on your account page, with USDT or another coin, or by card where your account page offers it. In the app, Settings, Plans, Buy data opens the bot set to recharge the account you are signed in to. Plans and prices are shown in the bot and on your account page after you sign in.

Can I pay with a bank card?

Yes, with a card that works on international websites. We never see or keep card details. Where your account page offers Pay by card, our card payment partner takes the payment on its own page. Otherwise a card works in two steps: it buys USDT at a service that sells crypto, and the USDT pays on your account page; the pay by card guide has the steps.

Something does not work. Where is help?

The setup guides have the steps for every device, and the help page has a troubleshooting checklist. You can also write in the chat on this page, or send /human to our Telegram bot.

Which devices does Albatross work on?

Albatross has an app for Windows 10 and 11, for Android 6 and newer (phones, and Android TV and TV boxes with the armv7 file), for Mac with an Apple chip or Intel, and for Linux on x86-64 or ARM64; they are all in Download. iPhone and iPad connect through a connection app from the App Store, set up from your account page. One account signs in on all of them, up to your plan's device limit.

Does it work on iPhone and iPad?

Yes, through a connection app from the App Store: there is no Albatross app for iPhone or iPad yet. Your account page hands your servers to the connection app (V2Box and Streisand are free), with one tap for all servers or one country's. They show up under the Albatross name and refresh by themselves. Add the page to your Home Screen and it opens like an app.

How do updates work?

The app checks for a new version when it starts and every few hours, downloads it, checks that it is the file we published and installs it in place. A required update stops older versions from connecting until they update.

Ready when you need it.

Install it once. On the day your network closes the usual doors, Albatross already knows the other ones.
