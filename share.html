Sharing

Use the VPN on another device.

A phone, laptop or tablet on the same Wi-Fi, or on your hotspot, can use the connection of a device where Albatross is on.

Steps

If a switch shows a lock, the app tells you which plan has it.

  1. Connect Albatross on your phone or computer.
  2. Connect the other device to the same Wi-Fi, or to this device's hotspot.
  3. In Albatross, open Settings and turn on Share with devices. A card shows an address in large type with a Copy button.
  4. On the other device, open its Wi-Fi settings, set Proxy to Automatic (also called Proxy auto-config or Use setup script) and enter that address.
  5. No automatic setting? Set the proxy by hand with the two values shown under the card.

Where to enter it

  • Android: Wi-Fi, long-press or tap the gear of your network, Edit or Advanced, Proxy, Proxy Auto-Config, and paste the address.
  • iPhone and iPad: Settings, Wi-Fi, the (i) next to your network, Configure Proxy, Automatic, and paste the address.
  • Windows: Settings, Network and internet, Proxy, Use setup script, and paste the address.
  • Mac: System Settings, Wi-Fi, Details next to your network, Proxies, Automatic proxy configuration, and paste the address.

Xbox and most TVs have no proxy setting: see TVs and consoles.
