Steps

Android 6 and newer. Nearly every phone from recent years is 64-bit and takes the usual APK; an old 32-bit phone takes the 32-bit APK (armv7). Albatross is not on Google Play, so your phone asks once to allow installs from your browser or from Telegram. You need an Albatross account: a username and a password, nothing else. Get a free trial account, or buy one.

  1. Get the app: tap Download the APK above. Or, in our Telegram bot, tap Get the app here in Telegram and the bot sends the file in the chat. On a slow connection, take the same file from GitHub.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If the phone does not allow unknown apps from your browser, tap Settings, allow your browser (or Telegram), go back and tap Install. If Google Play Protect warns about an app that is not from Google Play, tap More details, then Install anyway.
  3. Open Albatross and sign in with your username (not an email) and password. Paste the password exactly, with no space before or after it.
  4. Press the big button. When Android asks to allow the VPN, tap OK.
  5. Leave the location on Fastest location and the connection setting on Automatic.

Always-on VPN and other settings

  • Always-on VPN. In Albatross's Settings, tap Kill switch: it opens Android's own VPN settings. Turn on Always-on VPN so the VPN starts with the phone, and Block connections without VPN if nothing should go out while it is off.
  • Turn it off. Press the big button again, or turn the VPN off in Android's VPN settings.
  • Local apps outside the VPN. On Silver plans and up, Local apps outside the VPN lets your country's banking, payment, taxi and shopping apps work while you are connected.
  • “App not installed” can mean the phone is an old 32-bit one, which takes the 32-bit APK (armv7) instead, from the download page from version 1.3.6 on; or that an earlier Albatross version has to be removed first.
  • Later updates install from inside the app, over the one you have.
