Browsers

Albatross for your browser.

An extension for Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi and Firefox. It sends the browser through the Albatross app on the same computer, and lets you pick a location, set sites to open directly and turn protection on and off without leaving the page.

What you need

The Albatross app on the same computer (Windows, Mac or Linux), signed in. A browser cannot make the connection itself: the extension is the app's remote control.

Install it in Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera or Vivaldi

  1. Download the file above and unzip it: right-click it and choose Extract All (Windows) or double-click it (Mac). You get a folder named albatross-browser-1.2.0. Keep it where it is: the browser runs the extension from it.
  2. Open the extensions page: type chrome://extensions in the address bar (Edge: edge://extensions, Opera: opera://extensions) and press Enter.
  3. Turn on Developer mode (top right in Chrome; on the left in Edge).
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the folder from step 1.
  5. Click the puzzle icon in the toolbar and pin Albatross.

Pair it with the app

  1. Open the Albatross app and sign in.
  2. Click the Albatross icon in the browser's toolbar and choose Pair.
  3. Type the six-digit code the app shows. You pair once; the app lists paired browsers in Settings, Browsers, where you can remove one.
  4. Turn on Protect this browser. The icon shows the country you are connected through.
