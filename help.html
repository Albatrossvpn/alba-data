Set up Albatross.

Install, sign in, press the button. The steps for each device, and what to do when something does not work.

Android · Windows · iPhone and iPad · Mac and Linux · Troubleshooting

Before you start

You need an Albatross account: a username and a password, nothing else. Buy one, or add data to the one you have: in our Telegram bot with Telegram Stars, or on your account page with USDT or another coin, or by card where your account page offers it. Plans and prices are shown in our Telegram bot and on your account page after you sign in. Your account page also shows what is left of your data and when the account ends.

Android

Android guide on its own page. Android 6 and newer, on 64-bit phones (nearly every phone from recent years). Albatross is not on Google Play, so your phone asks once to allow installs from your browser.

  1. In the Download section, tap Download the APK. On a slow connection, take the same file from the GitHub link under the button.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If the phone says installs from this source are not allowed, tap Settings, allow your browser, go back and tap Install.
  3. Open Albatross and sign in. No account yet? Tap No account? Buy one in Telegram.
  4. Press the big button. When Android asks to allow the VPN, tap OK.
  5. Leave the location on Fastest location and the connection setting on Automatic.
  • “App not installed” can mean the phone is an old 32-bit one: it takes the 32-bit APK (armv7) instead, from the download page from version 1.3.6 on. TVs: see the Android TV guide.
  • Always-on VPN in the app's Settings opens the system's switches that keep the VPN on all the time.
  • On Silver plans and up, Local apps outside the VPN lets your country's banking, payment, taxi and shopping apps work while you are connected.
  • Later updates install from inside the app, over the one you have.

Windows

Windows guide on its own page. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.

  1. In the Download section, click Download for Windows (or the GitHub link on a slow connection).
  2. Run the installer. If Windows says Windows protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway: the installer is not code-signed yet.
  3. Open Albatross, sign in and click the big button.
  4. Leave the location on Fastest location and the connection setting on Automatic.
  • By default Albatross sets the system proxy, which browsers and most apps use. If a game or an app does not go through it, turn on Protect the whole device (Gold plans and up).
  • Do not run another VPN at the same time.
  • Every file has its SHA-256 fingerprint next to its download button; the Windows guide shows how to check it.

iPhone and iPad

There is no Albatross app in the App Store. Your servers go into a connection app from the App Store instead. iOS 15 or newer.

  1. Install a connection app from the App Store: V2Box or Streisand are free. Shadowrocket, Hiddify and sing-box work too.
  2. In Safari, open your account page and sign in.
  3. Optional: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, so the page opens like an app.
  4. On the account page, tap the button that adds your servers to your app: all servers or one country. The connection app opens; confirm.
  5. In the connection app, pick one of the servers named Albatross and connect. The first time, iOS asks to add a VPN configuration: tap Allow.
  • The servers refresh by themselves. If they stop working, refresh the Albatross subscription in the connection app, or add it again from the account page.

Mac and Linux

Mac and Linux have their own Albatross app. Download it in the Download section and follow the Mac guide or the Linux guide: a few steps, then sign in and press the button. On a Mac, a connection app from the Mac App Store (V2Box, Streisand, Hiddify or sing-box) works too, with the same steps as on iPhone.

Troubleshooting

It does not connect

Try these in order:

  1. Update to the newest version: the Update button in the app, or the newest file from the download section.
  2. Set the device's date and time to automatic.
  3. Leave the connection setting on Automatic.
  4. Choose another location, or Fastest location.
  5. Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data. On a heavily filtered network, give it up to a minute.
  6. Close and reopen the app, and make sure no other VPN is on.
  7. Check on your account page that the data is not used up and the account has not ended. If it has, add data in the Telegram bot.
  8. Still nothing? Send /human to our Telegram bot, or use the chat on this page, with the exact message you see, your device, Wi-Fi or mobile data, and the app version.
It is slow

Albatross measures the real speed of its servers and moves to a faster one by itself. If it stays slow, choose Fastest location or another location, switch networks, update the app and reconnect.

It says too many devices

Every device your plan allows is connected already. Disconnect or sign out on a device you are not using, or add data to move up to a plan with more devices: Bronze has 1, Silver 2, Gold 4.

A banking or taxi app does not open while connected

Local sites open directly, outside the VPN, through Bypass local sites, which is on from the start. For local apps on Android, turn on Local apps outside the VPN (Silver and up). On Bronze, disconnect while you use that app.

I forgot my password

If you bought the account in our Telegram bot, send /password to the bot from the same Telegram account. For any other account, send /human and our team will help. Never write a password in a chat.

The app asks me to update before it connects

That is a required update. Tap Update in the app, or install the newest file from the download section; your account and settings stay.
