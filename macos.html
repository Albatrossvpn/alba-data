Set up a VPN on a Mac.

Download the zip, move Albatross to Applications, and open it once with right-click. Then sign in and press one button.

Not sure which? Apple menu, About This Mac: “Chip: Apple M…” is an Apple chip, “Processor: Intel” is Intel.

To check a file, run shasum -a 256 and the file's name in Terminal: the result must match the SHA-256 above.

Steps

You need an Albatross account: a username and a password, nothing else. Buy one in our Telegram bot, or on your account page with USDT.

  1. Download the zip for your Mac (Albatross-<version>-macos-arm64.zip or -macos-x86_64.zip) and open it: it unpacks Albatross in Downloads.
  2. Drag Albatross to Applications.
  3. The first time, right-click (or Control-click) Albatross in Applications and choose Open, then Open again. Apple has not notarized the app yet, so a plain double-click is refused the first time. On newer macOS, if there is no Open button: open System Settings, Privacy and Security, and click Open Anyway next to Albatross.
  4. The window opens in your browser. Sign in with your username and password and press the big button.

Good to know

  • After the first time, Albatross opens like any other app. It has no Dock icon: its window is in your browser, and opening Albatross again brings the window back. Quit Albatross is in its settings.
  • Albatross sets the proxy of every network service (Wi-Fi, Ethernet) for browsers and apps that use it, and puts back what was there when you disconnect. Protect the whole device is not available on Mac yet.
  • Keep Albatross in Applications: run from Downloads, macOS moves it to a temporary place, and Start with the system and updates cannot work.
  • Do not run another VPN at the same time.
